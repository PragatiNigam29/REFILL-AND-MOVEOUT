# Refill & Move Out

### One batch moves out. The next moves in.

Refill & Move Out is a campus-first student marketplace that connects graduating students who want to sell or pass on their unused college essentials with incoming students looking for affordable second-hand items.

The platform creates a simple cycle of reuse within the campus community while making second-hand buying more convenient and transparent.

---

## Problem

Every year, students graduate and leave behind useful items such as:

- Books
- Study tables and chairs
- Calculators
- Cycles
- Hostel essentials
- Other college supplies

At the same time, incoming students need many of these items when starting college or hostel life.

However, students face three major problems:

1. Useful items remain unused after students move out.
2. Freshers often have to buy expensive new products.
3. Buyers cannot easily verify the visible condition of second-hand items before showing interest.

---

## Our Solution

Refill & Move Out creates a direct connection between students moving out and students moving in.

### For students moving out

Students can:

- Create a Move Out listing
- Add items they no longer need
- Upload item photographs
- Set price and condition
- Receive interest from other students

### For students moving in

Students can:

- Create a Need
- Specify the required category
- Set a maximum budget
- View matching available items
- Inspect item photographs using AI
- Express interest in suitable items

---

## Key Features

### Student Authentication
- Student signup
- Login and logout
- Personal dashboard

### Move Out
Graduating students can create a move-out listing and add multiple items.

### Item Listings
Each item can contain:

- Name
- Category
- Description
- Price
- Condition
- Image

### Move In / Need Creation
Students can describe what they need and specify their maximum budget.

### Smart Matching
The system finds suitable items using:

- Category
- Maximum budget
- Availability

### Express Interest
Students can express interest in an item.

Sellers can view students who have shown interest in their listings.

### AI Visual Item Inspection

The platform includes an AI-powered visual inspection feature.

A buyer can select:

**AI Inspect Item**

The uploaded image is analyzed for visible concerns such as:

- Possible scratches
- Cracks
- Dents
- Stains
- Visible wear
- Damaged or missing visible parts

Detected areas can be highlighted on the item image using bounding boxes, along with an AI-generated inspection summary.

> AI inspection is based only on visible information in the uploaded image and does not guarantee the absence of hidden defects.
