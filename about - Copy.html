ABOUT REFILL

Reuse what you no longer need. Refill.

What is Refill?

Refill is a student-focused second-hand marketplace designed to make campus move-ins and move-outs easier, more affordable, and more sustainable.

Every year, students moving out of hostels or college accommodation have books, furniture, electronics, hostel essentials, and other useful items that they no longer need.

At the same time, new and continuing students are looking for the same items at affordable prices. Refill connects these two sides of the campus community.

How Refill Works

Students can list items they no longer need, while other students can discover items that match their requirements and budget.

Buyers can express their interest in an item. Sellers can then accept or reject the request. Once a request is accepted, the seller can contact the interested buyer directly.

What We Offer

Student-to-Student Marketplace
Buy and sell useful items within the student community.

Move-Out Listings
Students moving out can list items they no longer need.

Move-In Needs
Students can post what they are looking for and discover matching items.

AI Visual Inspection
Our AI-powered inspection feature helps identify visible concerns such as scratches, dents, stains, cracks, or damaged parts in an item's image.

Interest & Contact System
Buyers can express interest and sellers can manage requests before connecting with each other.

Why Refill?

Instead of buying everything new and leaving useful belongings behind during move-out, Refill gives those items a second life.

It helps students save money, reduces unnecessary waste, and creates a more connected campus community.

Our Vision

Refill aims to build a more connected and sustainable campus ecosystem where one student's unused item can become another student's affordable solution.

We believe that reuse should be simple, affordable, and accessible to every student.
